LingoMusic Support

LingoMusic helps you learn English by listening to songs. Lyrics stay on screen; tap a word or line for a plain-language explanation. Song of the Day is free to start.

Account and deletion

Sign up with email (confirm the link we send) or Sign in with Apple. To delete your account and data, open the app and go to Me → Account → Delete account. Guest listening has no cloud account; use Clear data on this device.

Privacy

The full policy is also in the app under Me → About → Privacy Policy. We do not sell personal data. This launch is not on the China Mainland App Store.

Publisher

Shanghai BestCovered Limited. For other issues, use the support contact on the LingoMusic App Store product page, or the in-app Support screen (Me → About → Support).

LingoMusic Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

LingoMusic is a listen-to-learn app from Shanghai BestCovered Limited (iOS bundle ID com.nan.lingotify). You follow songs with lyrics on screen, tap words for explanations in your language, and optionally practice lines out loud.

Accounts

You can create an account with email (we send a confirmation link) or Sign in with Apple. A Google account is not required for this launch.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, optional name, and how you signed in.
  • Learning preferences you choose: language you are learning, interface/gloss language, level, goal, commute scene, and an optional age range (this is not identity verification).
  • On-device activity: liked songs, saved words and sentences, viewed explanations, and listen history. When you are signed in, vocabulary can sync to our database so it is not lost if you reinstall.
  • The operating system may send diagnostics if you opt in to share with developers.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal data. We do not use your account to show third-party ads. LingoMusic is not offered on the China Mainland App Store in this launch.

Payments

If you subscribe to LingoMusic Plus, Apple processes the purchase. We do not receive your full payment card number.

Children

LingoMusic is not directed at children under 13. Age range in the app is an optional learning preference.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep account and synced learning data until you delete your account. In the app, go to Me → Account → Delete account. That removes your LingoMusic user from our servers and clears likes, words, and settings on that device. Uninstalling the app alone does not delete the server account. Guest listening has no cloud account; clearing data only affects that device.

Sharing

We use Supabase to host authentication and synced vocabulary. Sign in with Apple is provided by Apple. We share data with these providers only to run the service.

Contact

Shanghai BestCovered Limited publishes LingoMusic. For privacy questions, use Support in the app (Me → About → Support), or the contact details on the LingoMusic App Store product page.
